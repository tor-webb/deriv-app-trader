Risk Management
Fixed always trades your chosen prediction. Follow Trend reads the last N ticks of this symbol and bets with whichever side (even/odd, high/low digits, hottest digit) has occurred most. Reversal does the opposite — betting the recent pattern won't continue. AI Analysis scores the deviation from expected uniform distribution (a z-score), gates on sample size and volatility, and only trades — as BUY/STRONG_BUY — once confidence clears your threshold; otherwise it holds at WAIT and rechecks on the interval above. It runs entirely on the live tick/digit data already streaming in this page — no external calls. Synthetic index digits are engineered to be close to uniformly random, so no strategy — including this one — guarantees wins; "WAIT" is a normal and often frequent outcome.
